Twensdaywhat can a growing matrix compute?

A point begins with many possible local computations and one finite structural budget. Repeated useful traffic does not merely change a number: it reallocates that budget, making some computations and recurrent routes structurally dominant while leaving a small reserve for alternatives.

The question changed.

Earlier work asked whether geometry, phase, source separation, or recurrent traffic could do something useful. Twensday asks a simpler engineering/mathematical question:

What family of operators does this constrained growth process actually produce?

So what matrices can it produce?

Linear generic-basis case. With fixed basis vectors rₖ and conserved masses mₖ ≥ 0,

w_eff = Σₖ mₖ rₖ,   Σₖ mₖ = 1

One point therefore chooses an effective row from the convex hull of its available basis directions. A population stacks those rows into a matrix. The growth rule determines where inside that constrained operator family the system spends its structural budget.

Relation-basis case. If the coordinates are explicit products AᵢBⱼ, the mass field itself is a sparse bilinear operator.

Nonlinear basis. With φ(rₖ·x), there is no single raw-space matrix anymore: the point is a constrained fixed-feature nonlinear model with trainable structural allocation.

Continuous/recurrent case. Some effective coefficients act on peer broadcasts. Stack several points and part of the grown operator becomes a recurrent matrix; its spectrum/gain determines whether activity dies, persists, oscillates, or becomes multistable.

What can it compute, already?

The earlier repo has individually earned these small verbs:

FITreceiver state changes what matters now
COMPOSEfixed structure + fast state selects nonlinear relations
CONTEXTpast input can alter later interpretation
CREDITeligibility lets delayed consequence reach vanished activity
ALLOCATEfinite growth turns utility into persistent sparse structure
RUNinternal state need not reset when output is emitted
RECURstate can live in circulating A↔B traffic
GROW LOOPrecurrent routes acquire mass only when persistence is useful
ADDRESSuseful traffic can be anonymous, but its address must stay stable
MIX BASISsemantic coordinates are unnecessary; stable generic coordinates suffice

Twensday should stop asking for another verb. It should map the operator families these ingredients produce: rank, sparsity, spectrum, attractors, context-dependent switching, nonlinear conjunctions, and population topology.

Two papers that changed the abstraction

Aizenbud et al., PNAS 2026
Dendritic morphology and synaptic nonlinearities enhance functional complexity in human cortical neurons. Leterrier, J. Neurosci. 2018
The Axon Initial Segment: An Updated Viewpoint.

These papers motivated extended/compartmentalized local computation and the separation between continuously evolving internal computation and an output/broadcast boundary. They do not validate the matrix equations or learning rule.

Twensday's first real research program

Instead of adding more biology, enumerate the computation:

Linear: Which matrices lie inside the basis-constrained convex hull? What ranks and singular spectra appear after growth?

Recurrent: Which sparse recurrent graphs grow for memory, switching, sequence, oscillator, and control tasks? What happens to spectral radius and attractor count?

Nonlinear: When do conjunctions genuinely require nonlinear local features, rather than a changed linear basis?

Plasticity: How do reserve and total capacity trade stability against reallocation?

Attackers: compare everything against least squares, sparse regression, ordinary RNNs, reservoirs, and matched DSP.

Twensday is a development notebook. Pretty matrices are not evidence. Every claimed new capability should still get a kill condition and a boring attacker.
